Privacy & Audit

Demo data
Design commitments for the MVP, aligned with Egypt Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020.
De-identification

Names, national IDs and phone numbers are removed before any AI analysis.

Encryption

Files are encrypted in transit and at rest.

Patient isolation

Each patient has a separate data space. A question never reads another patient.

No training

Patient files are not used to train models.
